4–16 letters or digits. Everyone at your party uses the same name.
How to play
  1. Two teams take turns eliminating one option at a time.
  2. Somewhere in the list hides the target — the worst / lowest / odd one out.
  3. Each safe elimination gives your team +1. Eliminating the target costs −4 and ends the round.
  4. If only the target is left, nobody hit it and the round is a tie.
Pick 1–10 rounds. Click order is play order.
